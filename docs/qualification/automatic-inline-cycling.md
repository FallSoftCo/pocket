Calm automatic inline immersion
==============================

The single existing prose flow remains primary. There are no translated subtitle lines, parallel paragraphs, gloss lanes or rescue modals. Ordinary Markdown bold/italic/link styling remains; grammar metadata is retained but automatic rainbow role colors, forced Medium weight and underlines are removed.

Reading evidence informs caution, not an invented optimum animation duration. [Larson's reading review](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/develop/word-recognition) explains fixation and next-word preview; this does not establish that a 200 ms UI animation is ideal. [Yantis and Jonides](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6238122/) found abrupt visual onsets can capture attention in visual search; this is not a test of our bilingual UI. [W3C pause/stop/hide guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html) supports control over distracting automatic updates. The 600 ms spatial handover and long holds are design hypotheses requiring native review.

Content now uses natural native paragraph shaping and whitespace. Placeholder word columns and artificial BoldItalic measurement are eliminated. Source dwell is 6–12 seconds; target dwell is at least18 seconds, increasing with content length. Initial message staggering is at most6 seconds. The whole visible message holds one variant set instead of independently blinking every phrase. A deliberate phrase tap holds its source inline; retapping resumes. Displayed speech freezes its exact captured mixed snapshot even after composition recreation.

Motion eligibility comes from the shared environment: resumed lifecycle, animations enabled, no touch exploration, no keyboard, and a quiet period after touch/scroll. Ineligibility holds the readable current snapshot rather than snapping on every motion-preference change. Reduced-motion initial content is target; manual rescue remains available. Timers have no per-frame parent updates.

The shared BlendImmersionText draws a complementary spatial handover while a single native snapshot supplies accessibility and link interaction. It never fades an entire paragraph to blank. Exact inherited fonts/style and line metrics are preserved. Native content reserves the maximum measured source/target paragraph height rather than independently padding every word.

Reservation tradeoff is explicit: unequal source/target languages cannot simultaneously preserve natural spaces, maximum width for each word, and identical line breaks. Natural typography is prioritized; horizontal word positions may change across languages while the surrounding vertical envelope remains reserved. Max source/target envelope is not a mathematical guarantee for every possible manually selected mixed-language combination; native inspection is required, especially with large font scales and bold/italic content.

Pure tests cover exact single-flow assembly, protected source, captured mixed speech recovery, overlap rejection, and minimum calm dwell. Old chunk decomposition helpers/tests remain harmless historical utilities; native renderer no longer uses their word slots. No claim of motion polish, native geometry or accessibility qualification is made before root's integrated Android review.
